Booking rules

read before you write to us
  1. Strictly 18+. Government ID at the door, no exceptions, no "my uncle is waiting outside."
  2. Blackwork only. We do not do colour, watercolour, or "just a small coloured butterfly." There are excellent colour studios in this city — we are not one.
  3. ₹2,000 booking deposit, adjusted against your final sitting. No-shows forfeit it to the studio cat's food fund.
  4. ₹3,000 per hour, timed from first line to last wipe. Flash pieces are fixed-price as marked.
  5. No cover-ups of work less than a year old. Fresh tattoos need to settle before anyone buries them.
