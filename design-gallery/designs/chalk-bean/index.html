— our story —

A café that erases itself.

Chalk & Bean started in 2021 when founder Arjun Bhandari — a former art teacher — painted his garage wall with chalkboard paint and started serving pour-overs to neighbours.

The rule stuck: if it's worth serving, it's worth lettering by hand. Our baristas train in chalk lettering alongside latte art. The board changes with the seasons, the mood, and whatever the market had fresh that morning.

Regulars have a tradition: photograph the board, tag us, and the best shot of the month gets redrawn permanently on the "hall of foam" wall.

House rules

  • Laptops welcome, calls outside please
