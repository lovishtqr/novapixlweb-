The Oven

Slow is the whole point.

Our day starts at 3 AM. Here's what happens before you smell anything.

01

Feed the starter

"Bubbles", our 6-year-old levain, gets fed stone-milled flour and warm water.

3:00 AM
02

Shape & score

Yesterday's fermented dough is shaped by hand and scored with the lame.

4:30 AM
03

Deck oven bake

Stone deck at 250°C, steam for the first 20 minutes for that blistered crust.

5:15 AM
04

Cool & shelf

Full cool-down on racks — slicing hot bread ruins the crumb, so we wait.

6:00 AM
