Where your cup has been

Twelve estates, roasted in-house every Tuesday. Ask the bar for the tasting flight — four origins, one passport stamp each.

01

Coorg, Karnataka

Chocolate-heavy, low acidity. Our house espresso. Estate: Pollibetta, 1,100 m.

02

Chikmagalur

Where Indian coffee began. Nutty, full-bodied pour-overs. Estate: Baba Budangiri.

03

Araku Valley

Tribal-grown arabica, floral and sweet. Best as a slow V60.

04

Yercaud, TN

Bright, citrusy lots for the moka pot crowd. Estate: MSP Plantations.

05

Coorg Peaberry

Rare single-bean lots, intense and syrupy. Limited weekly roast.

06

Ratnagiri Estate

Spice-forward, built for milk drinks. The cortado's best friend.
