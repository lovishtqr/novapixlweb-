aftercare, or the ink suffers

Second-skin stays on. Sleep on clean sheets. If it weeps, that is plasma, not a haunting — leave it alone.

Days 4–10 it will flake like old wallpaper. Do not pick. Moisturise thin, twice daily, with the balm we hand you.

Four weeks of no direct sun, no tanning, no "just five minutes." After that: SPF 50 forever. The crypt protects its own.

One free touch-up within a year. Bring the healed piece, we judge it honestly, we fix what needs fixing.
