॥ अभ्यास ॥ — TRAINING

The daily dangal of discipline.

Four sessions a day. Mud-pit bouts every Sunday morning — spectators welcome.

5:00 – 7:00 AM

Pit Session

Mud-pit kushti drills, bodyweight vyayam, and rope climbing. The earth is your equipment.

7:00 – 8:30 AM

Mugdar & Gada

Wooden club and mace swinging — shoulder mobility and grip forged over hundreds of reps.

5:00 – 7:00 PM

Nal & Stone

Stone weight lifting: the traditional nal, sumo-style deadlifts, and carries across the yard.

SUN 7:00 AM

Sunday Dangal

Friendly bouts in the pit. Win or learn — the whole akhara cheers either way.
