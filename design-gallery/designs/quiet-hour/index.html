Etiquette

The house rules of quiet

  1. Silent hours, 2–4 PM daily: no talking anywhere in the bathhouse. Whispers carry; we ask you not to test this.
  2. Phones stay in the cedar lockers at the entrance. There is no signal in the baths, by design.
  3. Move slowly between waters. Running is for the world outside the gate.
  4. The 60-minute soak is included with every therapy — it is not a waiting room, it is the treatment.
  5. Children under 14 are welcome only in the Pine Court, before noon.
