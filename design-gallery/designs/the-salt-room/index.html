Journal

Notes on slowness.

October 2026

Why we removed the fourth ritual.

For a year we offered four rituals. Guests chose faster, not better. So we took one away. Nobody has asked for it back — they ask, instead, to stay ten minutes longer in the salt room. We always say yes.

September 2026

The pine oil comes from one grove.

A single deodar grove above Dharampur supplies our oil. When the monsoon washes the path out, we simply wait. Some months the Pine Silence smells a little different. We think that is honest.
