Silver cascade, foil-lighted · Studio study Nº 14

Silver is not the absence of colour.

For decades the industry sold you coverage — dye the grey, hide the years. We believe the opposite: grey hair has depth, dimension and light that no tube of dye can imitate. Our work is blending, toning and cutting so your silver reads as intentional.

Every plan begins with a strand test and a grow-out map. No two silvers are alike; neither are our formulas.
