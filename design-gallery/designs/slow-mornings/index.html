The ritual

How a slow morning goes

Four small ceremonies. No rush between them.

7:30

The first pour

The slow bar opens with a single-origin pour-over of the day — Coorg or Chikmagalur, ground at 7 AM sharp. First cup is for the smell.

8:15

Pen to paper

Borrow a fountain pen and one of our house journals. Three pages, no editing. Staff will never read over your shoulder — house rule.

9:40

Second cup, slower

Cortado or filter kaapi, served in ceramic with a shortbread thumbprint. Conversation allowed now — quietly.

11:00

The world returns

Laptops open, phones unmuted, the day begins. You leave with ink on your fingers and a page you didn't know you needed.
