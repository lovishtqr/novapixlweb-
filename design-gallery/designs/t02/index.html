Aftercare

don't mess it up

Keep the second-skin on for 72 hours unless it leaks. When it comes off, wash with lukewarm water and fragrance-free soap. Pat dry — never rub.

Thin layer of the balm we give you, twice a day. Do not scratch, do not pick, do not "just check" the scab. It itches because it's healing — congratulations.

No swimming, no gym sweat-sessions, no sunbathing. After 30 days: sunscreen forever. Your tattoo's enemy is UV, not us.

One free touch-up within 6 months if anything heals patchy. After that it's ₹1,000 a sitting — still cheap, still loud.
