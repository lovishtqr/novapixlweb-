The oath

aftercare, sworn
  1. I will keep it wrapped for the hours my artist names, and wash it like it owes me money — gently.
  2. I will not swim, tan, or gym-sweat for three weeks. The forge needs cooling time.
  3. I will moisturise thin, never thick. A drowning tattoo heals like a drowning anything.
  4. I will return for check-ups at two weeks and two months — both free, both non-negotiable.
  5. I will wear sunscreen on it forever. Iron rusts in the sun; so does ink.
